I am learning the theory on game engines while im building my own game engine, 
but im having trouble making decisions about the design. Like, how ill build a material system.
I also dont know how big this project will be so its hard to estimate how complex I should make the subsystems. 
Do you have any tips?



Building a game engine can be a challenging and rewarding task, but it can also be overwhelming due to the complexity involved. 
Here are a few tips that might help you with your design decisions:

1. Define the scope of your project: Before you start designing your game engine, 
it's important to have a clear idea of what your project entails. 
This will help you to determine the level of complexity that your subsystems should have. Consider the type of game you're building, 
the platforms you're targeting, the features you want to include, and the size of your team.

2. Look at existing game engines: You can learn a lot by studying the design of existing game engines. 
Look at the architecture of popular engines like Unity, Unreal Engine, and CryEngine to see how they've implemented their subsystems. 
Take note of the features they include, how they're organized, and how they're interconnected.

3. Consider modularity: A modular design will make it easier to expand your engine in the future. 
You can break down your engine into separate subsystems that can be developed and tested independently. 
This will make it easier to manage the complexity of your engine and make it more flexible.

4.Use patterns and best practices: There are well-established design patterns and best practices that 
you can use to guide your engine's design. For example, the Model-View-Controller pattern can help you to separate your engine's logic, 
rendering, and user interface. Using established patterns can make your code more readable and easier to maintain.

5.Build in flexibility: It's hard to predict all the ways that your engine will be used, 
so it's important to build in flexibility. Make sure that your subsystems can be configured and customized to suit different needs.
This will make your engine more versatile and useful to a wider range of developers.

6.Keep it simple: It's easy to get carried away with designing a complex system, 
but it's important to keep things simple. A simple, elegant design will be easier to understand, maintain, and extend. 
Avoid over-engineering your system by focusing on what's necessary to achieve your project's goals.

Remember that building a game engine is a complex undertaking that requires a lot of 
planning and attention to detail. With a clear understanding of your project's scope and goals,
and a careful consideration of your subsystem designs, you can create a powerful and flexible engine that will serve you well in the future.
